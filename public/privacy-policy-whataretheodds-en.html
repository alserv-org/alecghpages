Last updated: July 25, 2026

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Data stored only on Your Device

The following information is created by playing the game and is written to the Application's local storage on Your Device. The Company does not receive it, and it is not transmitted to any server operated by the Company:

  • Which difficulty tiers You have unlocked.
  • Your remaining attempts and when they last regenerated.
  • Your play statistics: total attempts, wins, closest guess, per-tier records and day streak.
  • Your settings, such as sound and vibration preferences.
  • A cached copy of the most recent Leaderboard You loaded, so it can be shown while offline.
  • A pending score, if You won a round while offline, so it can be submitted to the Leaderboard once You reconnect.

This information is deleted when You uninstall the Application, and can be cleared at any time from the in-game Settings → Reset progress. If You have Android's Auto Backup enabled on Your Device, Android may copy the Application's local storage to Your own Google Drive backup. That backup is a Google service governed by Google's terms and is not accessible to the Company.

Personal Data

The Application does not ask You to provide Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. The Personal Data associated with the Service is limited to:

  • the Play Games Services profile information described below, if and only if You choose to sign in; and
  • the identifiers and usage information that AdMob collects when an ad is requested or shown.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically by the advertising and Play Games components of the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), Your Advertising ID and other device identifiers, the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile operating system and its version, coarse or approximate location derived from Your IP address, and information about Your interaction with an advertisement (for example that an ad was requested, shown, closed, or clicked).

Google Play may also collect information about the Application independently of this Privacy Policy — for example crash and performance information that Google Play provides to all developers in aggregated form. That collection is governed by Google's own terms.

Leaderboards and Google Play Games Services

Signing in to Play Games Services is optional. The Application is fully playable without signing in, and if You never sign in, no score or profile information is sent to a Leaderboard.

If You do sign in, the following information is processed through Google:

  • Your Play Games player ID, display name and avatar image — used to identify Your entry on the Leaderboard and to highlight Your own row.
  • Your scores — the best result You have achieved is submitted to the Leaderboard, where it is visible to other players of the Application together with Your Play Games display name and avatar. A score won while offline is stored on Your Device and submitted the next time You are online.
  • Achievement unlocks — recorded against Your Play Games profile when You clear a difficulty tier.
  • Your Play Games friends list — accessed only if You explicitly grant friends access when prompted by Google, and used only to display the friends Leaderboard inside the Application.

A Leaderboard is public to other players by design. Do not sign in with a Play Games display name that You do not want other players to see. You can change Your display name or avatar, or hide Your profile, in the Google Play Games app.

This data is hosted and controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The Application receives it from Google's SDK in order to draw the Leaderboard screen, caches the most recently loaded Leaderboard on Your Device so it can be shown offline, and does not copy it to any other server. The Company has no ability to read Your Google account beyond what the Play Games SDK returns.

The Application also offers a share button that hands Your result to Android's standard share sheet. Nothing is shared unless You tap it and choose a destination app, and the content shared is the text You see in the share sheet. What happens to it afterwards is governed by the privacy policy of the app You chose.

Advertising

The Application shows Rewarded Ads served by Google AdMob. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt You without Your action: an ad is shown only after You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

To serve those ads, Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects and may use the Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location and ad interaction data to select ads, measure their performance, limit how often the same ad is shown, and detect invalid traffic and fraud. Where You have consented to it, that data may also be used for personalized advertising.

The Application requests ads with a maximum ad content rating of "G". The Android permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is declared because the advertising SDK reads the Advertising ID.

Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Company does not receive Your Personal Data from Google. The Company receives only aggregated reporting, such as the number of ads shown and the revenue earned.

Consent for advertising

The Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain the consent required by law in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where consent is required, a Google-provided consent form is presented before any ad is requested, no ad request is made until You have answered it, and Your choice is stored and applied to subsequent ad requests. In regions where consent is not legally required, ads are requested without a consent form.

You can limit or reset advertising identifiers at any time from Your Device: Settings → Google → Ads, where Android lets You reset Your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization. Where Google requires a persistent privacy options entry point for Your region, the consent form can be presented again through the Application.

In-app purchases

The Application contains no in-app purchases. No payment information is ever requested or processed by the Application.
