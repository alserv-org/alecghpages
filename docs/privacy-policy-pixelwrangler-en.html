Privacy Policy — Pixel Wrangler

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

The short version: Pixel Wrangler has no account and no server run by the developer. Your progress stays on Your device. The only personal information that leaves Your device is what Google's advertising SDK collects when the game shows a rewarded ad.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Data stored only on Your Device

The following information is created by playing the game and is written to the Application's local storage on Your Device. The Company does not receive it, and it is not transmitted to any server operated by the Company:

  • Your run and meta progression: shards earned and spent, lifetime shards, best wave reached, best score, number of runs played and number of full clears.
  • The levels of the permanent upgrades You have purchased with in-game currency.
  • Your settings, such as sound and haptic feedback preferences.
  • Tutorial state, so the tutorial is not repeated.
  • Internal save-format information used to migrate saves between versions of the Application.

This information is deleted when You uninstall the Application. If You have Android's Auto Backup enabled on Your Device, Android may copy the Application's local storage to Your own Google Drive backup. That backup is a Google service governed by Google's terms and is not accessible to the Company.

Personal Data

The Application does not ask You to create an account and does not ask You to provide Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. The Personal Data associated with the Service is limited to the identifiers and usage information that AdMob collects when an ad is requested or shown, as described below.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically by the advertising components of the Service when an ad is requested or shown.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), Your Advertising ID and other device identifiers, the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile operating system and its version, coarse or approximate location derived from Your IP address, and information about Your interaction with an advertisement (for example that an ad was requested, shown, closed, or clicked).

Google Play may also collect information about the Application independently of this Privacy Policy — for example crash and performance information that Google Play provides to all developers in aggregated form. That collection is governed by Google's own terms.

Advertising

The Application shows Rewarded Ads served by Google AdMob. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt You without Your action: an ad is shown only after You choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

To serve those ads, Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects and may use the Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location and ad interaction data to select ads, measure their performance, limit how often the same ad is shown, and detect invalid traffic and fraud. Where You have consented to it, that data may also be used for personalized advertising.

Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The Company does not receive Your Personal Data from Google. The Company receives only aggregated reporting, such as the number of ads shown and the revenue earned.

Consent for advertising

The Application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain the consent required by law in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where consent is required, a Google-provided consent form is presented before ads are requested, and Your choice is stored and applied to subsequent ad requests. In regions where consent is not legally required, ads are requested without a consent form.

You can limit or reset advertising identifiers at any time from Your Device: Settings → Google → Ads, where Android lets You reset Your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization. Where Google requires a persistent privacy options entry point for Your region, the consent form can be presented again through the Application.

Leaderboards, online scores and social features

As of the "Last updated" date above, the Application has no leaderboards, no online score submission, no achievements, no multiplayer and no sign-in. Your scores, best wave and statistics are kept on Your Device only and are never sent to the Company, to Google Play Games Services, or to any other player.

If a future version of the Application adds a leaderboard or another online feature, that version will require the relevant sign-in or opt-in, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the feature ships, and the "Last updated" date will change. Any leaderboard would necessarily publish the score and the display name You choose to other players.

In-app purchases

The Application contains no in-app purchases. All in-game currency and upgrades are earned by playing. No payment information is ever requested or processed by the Application.
